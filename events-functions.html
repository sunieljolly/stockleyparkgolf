The Marquee at Stockley Park

One of the premier event venues in Middlesex and the home counties, for 150 to 650 guests.

Host the perfect event

A party, wedding, christening, engagement or anniversary — or a large, flexible space for a corporate event. The marquee adapts to the occasion.

As a blank canvas, the space lets organisers apply their own branding or theme, so guests get the experience you intended rather than a template. The rolling parkland and lake provide the backdrop and the photographs.

Occasions we host

From a private celebration to a full-scale conference, the space and the team flex to fit.

Weddings & celebrations

Weddings, christenings, engagements and anniversaries, with the parkland and lake for photographs.

Galas & awards

Dinner dances, charity galas and awards ceremonies, with room for stages, screens and branding.

Corporate & trade

Trade shows, press conferences, product launches, seminars and conferences, with an outdoor terrace.

What the venue includes

Practical, built-in features that remove the usual headaches of dressing an empty space.

  • Private entrance
  • Free private car park
  • Temperature-controlled
  • Air conditioning
  • Outdoor terrace with optional bar
  • Private bar available
  • LED starlit ceiling
  • Views over the tree-lined course

Who to speak to

The Stockley Marquee is fully managed by Venue Management Solutions Ltd, who handle everything from quotations and viewings through to bookings, payments and the running of the day itself.

Contact them directly — they will take you through availability, capacity and what is included.
