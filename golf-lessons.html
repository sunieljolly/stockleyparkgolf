Golf lessons

Fifty years of combined coaching experience, applied to the part of your game that is costing you shots.

Coaching that targets your score

Our professionals are fully qualified members of the PGA and certified ColourPath Golf instructors, and both specialise in bespoke practice programmes and scoring performance.

Between them they have helped thousands of golfers of all ages and abilities see permanent improvement — whether that is a grip and stance rebuild, or the game management that turns a good swing into a good score.

Three ways to improve

One-to-one lessons

The focus is entirely yours. From grip to stance to swing, working on what you want to change and taking it back out onto the course.
