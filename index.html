Robert Trent Jones Senior The course opened to the public in 1993 and has been a fixture of west London golf ever since.

Championship quality, all year round

Our picturesque, challenging 18-hole par-72 course extends over 6,393 yards of mature, tree-lined fairways — and it plays through the winter too.

Originally designed by the renowned architect Robert Trent Jones Senior, the course sits in rolling parkland a short drive from Heathrow, with easy access to the M25 and junction 4 of the M4.

  • 'All-year-round' course with excellent drainage
  • No temporary tees — and proper greens through the winter
  • Buggies available for hire
  • Newly refurbished bar and restaurant in the clubhouse
