Privacy policy

How Stockley Park Golf Club Limited collects, uses and protects your personal data.

This website is owned and managed by Stockley Park Golf Club Limited. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy. If you have questions about how we use your personal data, contact us and we will assist.

Definitions

  • Personal data — any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person.
  • Processing — any operation performed on personal data.
  • Data subject — a natural person whose personal data is processed.
  • Child — a natural person under 16 years of age.
  • We / us — Stockley Park Golf Club Limited.

Data protection principles

  • Processing is lawful, fair and transparent, with lawful grounds and your rights considered first.
  • Processing is limited to the purpose for which data was gathered.
  • Processing uses the minimal amount of data required.
  • Data is not stored longer than needed.
  • We do our best to ensure data is accurate, integral and confidential.

Your rights

  • Right to information about whether and how your data is processed.
  • Right to access a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification or erasure of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Right to object to processing, including direct marketing.
  • Right to object to automated processing and profiling.
  • Right to data portability in a machine-readable format.
  • Right to lodge a complaint, and to seek help from a supervisory authority.
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time.

Access, correct or delete your information

Request by email: opsmanager@stockleygroup.com

Request by post: Stockley Park Golf Club, The Clubhouse, Stockley Park, Uxbridge, Middlesex, UB11 1AQ.

The data we gather

Information you provide

Your email address, name, billing address and telephone number — information necessary to deliver a product or service, or to improve your experience. This includes details you provide to comment or take part in activities on the site.

Information collected automatically

Information stored by cookies and other session tools, such as your IP address, browsing history and, where applicable, shopping basket information. This is used to improve your experience. When you use our services or view our site, your activity may be logged.

How we use your data

We use personal data to provide our services, register accounts, provide products and services you have ordered, send promotional items you have asked for, communicate with you about those products and services, and notify you of changes to services. We also use it to enhance your experience and to fulfil legal or contractual obligations.

On the grounds of entering a contract we process data to identify you, provide a service, communicate for sales or invoicing, arrange delivery, and provide order and booking updates.

On the grounds of legitimate interest we process data to send personalised offers, analyse our client base to improve the quality and availability of our services, and to conduct satisfaction questionnaires. Unless you tell us otherwise, we consider offers similar to your previous purchases to be a legitimate interest.

With your consent we process data to send newsletters and campaign offers, and for other purposes we have asked consent for.

We may anonymise data and use it outside the scope of this policy only once anonymised. We hold information as long as necessary to comply with statutory and contractual obligations and our legitimate interests as a data controller.

Who else can access your data

We do not share your personal data with strangers. We share it with trusted partners where needed to provide a service or improve your experience:

  • Processing partners: Chrono Golf and Lightspeed.
  • Connected third parties: Google Analytics and Facebook Business Manager.
  • Government bodies or third parties for approval, certification, research and statistical purposes.

We only work with processing partners able to ensure an adequate level of protection. We disclose data to third parties or public officials where legally obliged, where you have consented, or where there is another legal ground.

How we secure your data

We use safe protocols for communication and transferring data, and the site is protected by a third-party SSL certificate. We anonymise and pseudonymise where suitable, and monitor our systems for vulnerabilities and attacks. We monitor the use and content of emails, calls and secure messages so we can identify and act on unlawful or improper use of our systems.

We cannot guarantee the security of information, but we will notify suitable authorities of data breaches and notify you where there is a threat to your rights or interests.

Children

We do not intend to collect, or knowingly collect, information from children, and we do not target children with our services.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to analyse customer behaviour, administer the site, track users’ movements and collect information about users, in order to personalise and enhance your experience. You can control cookies at browser level; disabling them may affect some site functions.

  • Necessary cookies — required for features such as logging in. They collect no personal information.
  • Functionality cookies — remember choices such as your name and email in forms.
  • Analytics cookies — track use and performance of the site and services.
  • Advertising cookies — deliver relevant advertising and limit how often you see an advert.

You can remove cookies via your browser settings, or control some third-party cookies using a privacy enhancement platform such as optout.aboutads.info or youronlinechoices.com. For more information visit allaboutcookies.org.

We use Google Analytics to measure traffic. Google has its own privacy policy, and you can opt out via the Google Analytics opt-out page.

Email newsletter

This website operates an email newsletter program used to inform subscribers about products and services. Subscriptions are taken in compliance with the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003. Details are held securely and are not passed to third parties outside the company except as specified in this policy. Email campaigns may contain tracking facilities, and subscriber activity may be tracked to refine future campaigns. Subscribers can unsubscribe at any time via the automated system detailed in each campaign, or by following instructions provided instead.

Social media platforms

Communication and actions taken on external social media platforms are subject to those platforms’ own terms and privacy policies. We will never ask for personal or sensitive information through social media, and encourage you to use our primary channels for sensitive matters. Social sharing buttons may track and save your sharing request through your account.
